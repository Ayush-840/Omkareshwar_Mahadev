# Committee Admin Guide (one page)

Give this to the 2 or 3 admins. Translate it into Hindi before sharing.

## Open the admin area

1. Open the app.
2. Tap the temple logo 7 times on the home screen (or use the link you were given).
3. Log in with your email and password. Do not share your password.

## Post a notice

1. Tap **New notice**.
2. Write the title and message in Hindi (English is optional).
3. Add a photo if you want.
4. Turn on **Pin** to keep it at the top.
5. Turn on **Send notification** only for important news. Everyone with the app will get it.
6. Tap **Publish** and confirm.

## Add an event

1. Tap **New event**.
2. Fill title, date, time, place, short description. Add a photo.
3. Publish. People can add it to their calendar.

## Upload photos

1. Tap **Upload photos**.
2. Pick an album or create one (for example "Hanuman Jayanti 2027").
3. Choose photos (up to 20 at a time). Wait for the upload bar to finish.
4. Add a caption if useful.

## Update aarti timings or temple info

Ask [developer name, phone] until the edit screen for these is added. Do this when timings change for a season or festival.

## Good practice

- Post only temple-approved content; no personal photos without permission.
- Keep notices short: what, when, where.
- Avoid sending more than 2 notifications a week unless urgent.
- Check the app after publishing to see it appears.
- If something looks wrong, delete the item and tell [developer name].

## Problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Cannot log in | Check email and password; ask developer to reset |
| Upload stuck | Check internet, try fewer or smaller photos |
| Wrong notice sent | Delete the notice and post a correction |
| Lost phone | Tell the developer to disable your account |
